## Highlights

This release builds on v0.1.0 with a **self-contained vendored family stack**, a **locked/resumable job runner** (`sandbox run`), **Modal L4 benchmark runbooks**, and a **live-or-loud** reliability sweep across CLI, eval, and deploy surfaces.

### Family pins (offline by default)

- **llm-mailroom v0.7.1** and **llm-dojo-scoring v0.15.0** tracked under `vendor/` with drift guards (`tests/test_vendor_drift.py`).
- Corpus pin **v9 `46a4d3c2`** on `Lucius-Morningstar/mailroom-dataset` (3,302 rows).

### Job runner & Modal serving

- Spec-driven **`sandbox run`** (preflight lock, checkpoints, resume, Modal worker in `deploy/modal_job.py`).
- Modal vLLM **v0.29.0** + SDK **1.5.5**, HF secret wiring, teardown script, and L4 specialist suite (`run-30-*-specialist.yaml`).
- **Two-operator tracks** (`track-a` / `track-b`), **benchmark-check**, and **estimate-suite** cost CLI.
- Dedicated **`merger_agreement_specialist`** (no longer routed through contracts).

### Data & eval

- **`sandbox datasets pull`** — full Hub cache locally; **`datasets sample --per-class N`** for offline class quotas.
- Subclass-stratified strata (DMR-066), ModernBERT comparison hooks, serving metrics parity (`sandbox metrics compare --fixture`).

### Governance & packaging

- Sandbox-isolated **`SAND-*`** board (`governance/TASKS.md`).
- Complete **`[pipeline]`** / Modal job deps; `requirements/` mirrors pyproject extras.

### Quality

- **370** network-free tests (with `[pipeline]` extra); live-or-loud pins in `tests/test_live_or_loud_sweep.py`.
