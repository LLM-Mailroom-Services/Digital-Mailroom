## Provenance notes

- `gpu` (1x/2x) is parsed from the `job_id` token; blank when the id does not encode a GPU count.
- `n` is the job record `total` (the leg's document count); `done!=total` is flagged in `notes`.
- `driver_or_runner` names the series runner the inputs attribute to that run_id family (`scripts/run_a100_matrix.py`, `scripts/run_a100_v2.py`, `scripts/run_a100_v3_finish.py`); blank where no runner is identifiable.
- `config_spec` is the run-spec yaml the series runner cites, inferable from the run_id arm; blank where not inferable.
- `gpu_cost_usd` / `cost_per_doc` are taken from the A100-Research-Report.md base matrix (GPU $ per 100 docs) for the ten base allclass legs only; all other legs have no per-run cost source in the inputs.
- `score` and `dataset_fingerprint` are blank throughout: the staged `experiment_log_head.jsonl` and `reports/*.serving.json` carry only non-A100 (L4 / sand032 / sorter) legs, and `cards/A100-MASTER-SCORE-COST-CARD.md` is absent from the inputs.
