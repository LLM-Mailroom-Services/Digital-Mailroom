Key findings

  1. Insurance claims extract best (0.688); correspondence is at 0.300 (0.304 on a repeat run) on the SAND-032 posture.
  2. The frozen L5 serving config cut wall time 43.6% and cost per document 43.5% against the L0 baseline at equal quality (0.2841 vs 0.2787), with throughput up from 1,261 to 2,238 tok/s.
  3. A second L4 replica ran 2.06× faster at flat cost per document ($0.000211 vs $0.000205) on 100 correspondence documents.
  4. Merger agreements remain unsolved. A MAUD-specific prompt doubled per-question accuracy from 4.0% to 8.5%, still far from usable. Contracts reach 0.596 CUAD clause-detection F1.
  5. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash tops 2 of 5 API extraction tasks (insurance 0.797) for $0.171 across all five, about half the Qwen3-8B ($0.324) and Granite ($0.349) cost.
  6. ModernBERT Arm B is the first checkpoint to clear the 95% doc-type gate (95.05%, up from 89.47% for run-3). Conditional subclass accuracy is 58.3%, below its 75% gate.
  7. 8 source defects were found and documented, including an invalid held-out-plus eval and a coverage-field bug in the ModernBERT eval exporter. See Data quality.

ML diagnostics

Exploratory analysis and Accumulated Local Effects (ALE) for the two models the mailroom depends on: the ModernBERT intake classifier and the Qwen3-8B-AWQ specialists. ALE is computed on transparent surrogates fitted to the per-document results (ridge or logistic regression with quadratic and interaction terms), with 90% bootstrap bands from 200 refits and a 5-fold cross-validated fit score on every chart. A surrogate with no predictive skill is flagged, and its curve should not be read as an effect.
